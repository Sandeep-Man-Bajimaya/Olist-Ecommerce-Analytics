# Olist E-Commerce Analytics



## Project Overview



### 1\. Project Background

This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand sales performance, customer behavior, seller performance, product category performance, and delivery outcomes.

The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:



Raw Data
↓
MySQL
↓
SQL Data Preparation \& Analytical Views
↓
Business Analysis Queries
↓
Power BI
↓
Interactive Business Dashboard



The objective is to demonstrate practical data analyst skills rather than simply produce visualizations.



### 2\. Business Problem

An e-commerce business generates large volumes of transactional data across orders, customers, products, sellers, payments, deliveries, and reviews.



Raw transactional data does not directly answer important business questions such as:

* How much revenue does the business generate?
* Which product categories contribute the most revenue?
* Which sellers generate the most revenue?
* How concentrated is revenue across sellers and categories?
* How frequently do customers purchase?
* How long do customers remain active?
* What proportion of deliveries are late?
* Which areas require additional business attention?



This project transforms the underlying transactional data into reusable analytical datasets and business-focused insights.



### 3\. Objectives

The project objectives are to:

* Prepare delivered-order data for analysis.

### 5\. Data Preparation

The data was prepared in MySQL using reusable SQL views.

##### vw\_completed\_sales



Creates the main completed-sales dataset by combining:

* Orders
* Customers
* Order items
* Products
* Product category translations



Only orders with the status delivered are included.



##### vw\_completed\_orders

Extracts distinct completed order IDs from vw\_completed\_sales.

This view provides a reusable order-level filter for downstream analytical views.



##### vw\_category\_performance

Aggregates product-category performance using:

* Product count
* Order count
* Units sold
* Revenue
* Revenue share
* Average selling price

##### 

##### vw\_customer\_performance

Aggregates customer-level behavior using:

* Orders
* Revenue
* Average order value
* First order date
* Last order date
* Customer lifetime days



##### vw\_delivery\_performance

Classifies deliveries as:

* Early
* On Time
* Late



The classification compares actual customer delivery dates with estimated delivery dates.



##### vw\_seller\_performance

Aggregates seller-level performance using:

* Orders
* Units sold
* Revenue
* Average selling price



##### vw\_seller\_segments

Ranks sellers by revenue and divides them into four performance groups:

* Top Performer
* Strong Performer
* Developing Seller
* Low Performer



### 6\. SQL Analysis

Six standalone analysis queries were created in addition to the reusable views.



##### Category Performance

Evaluates category-level revenue, sales volume, and average selling price.



##### Category Revenue Concentration

Measures the proportion of total revenue generated by the highest-ranked categories.



##### Delivery Performance

Measures delivery outcomes and their share of completed deliveries.



##### Customer Performance

Analyzes customer revenue, order frequency, average order value, and customer lifetime.



##### Seller Performance

Compares sellers by revenue, order volume, units sold, and average selling price.



##### Seller Segmentation

Measures the number of sellers and revenue contribution of each seller performance segment.

### 9\. Key Findings



##### Delivery Performance

Of the completed deliveries analyzed:

* **91.89%** were Early.
* **1.34%** were On Time.
* **6.77%** were Late.

This indicates that the majority of analyzed deliveries arrived before the estimated delivery date.



##### Seller Revenue Concentration

Seller segmentation shows substantial concentration of revenue:

|Segment|Sellers|Revenue Share|
|-|-:|-:|
|Top Performer|594|82.29%|
|Strong Performer|891|14.40%|
|Developing Seller|891|2.93%|
|Low Performer|594|0.38%|



The 594 Top Performer sellers represent approximately 20% of the seller population while generating 82.29% of total seller revenue.



##### Customer Behavior

The customer-performance analysis shows:

* 93,358 unique customers.
* Average orders per customer of approximately 1.03.
* Total customer-level revenue of 13.22M.

The low average order frequency indicates that most customers have relatively limited repeat purchasing within the analyzed dataset.



##### Category Performance

Category analysis measures revenue, orders, units sold, products, revenue share, and average selling price.



The revenue concentration analysis divides categories into ranked groups to identify the contribution of the highest-performing categories.



### 10\. Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings:



##### Focus on High-Value Sellers

The concentration of revenue among Top Performer sellers suggests that these sellers are strategically important to the marketplace.

The business should monitor their performance, availability, and operational reliability.



##### Improve Seller Development

Developing and Low Performer sellers contribute relatively little revenue compared with the Top Performer group.

Seller enablement programs could focus on improving product availability, sales performance, and marketplace participation.



##### Monitor Late Deliveries

Although late deliveries represent a minority of completed deliveries, they remain an important operational KPI.

Late deliveries should be monitored by location, seller, and time period where sufficient data is available.



##### Increase Customer Repeat Purchases

The average order frequency is close to one order per customer.

Customer retention and repeat-purchase strategies could therefore represent an opportunity for increasing customer lifetime value.
